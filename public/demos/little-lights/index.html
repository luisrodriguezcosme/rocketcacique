About this demo

This is a playable mockup, not the finished app. It shows how a child moves through a lesson: watch the story, learn the word and sign, play two short games, then do it for real at home and earn a sticker.

  • All four lessons work at Level 1. Switch the level in the bar above to see the games change. The Share game shows the biggest difference.
  • Nothing has a timer and nothing buzzes. If a child waits 6 seconds, Lumi repeats the question. After 12 seconds, the right answer glows.
  • Lines are read out loud when a screen opens, and again with the yellow speaker. In this public demo the voice comes from your own browser, so it may sound different on each device. The real app will use recorded human voices.
  • Stickers stay in this browser only. Reset them in the Grown-up corner (press and hold the dashed button for 1 second).
  • All art is placeholder. Sign drawings need review by a signing teacher.
