Puerto Rico Crush

One day on the island. Work, chores, Papi y Mami, and way too many mosquitoes.

  • 1Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap where you want to go.
  • 2Stand at a task to do it. Each bubble has a timer ring. Green is fine. Red means hurry.
  • 3Zap mosquitoes with Space or the Zap button. Zap fast for a combo.
  • 4Some tasks need an item. Eggs from the nest, water from the barrel, cafecito from the kitchen.
