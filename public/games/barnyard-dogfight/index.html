      Arrow keys work like the nose and bank keys. You can also drag with the mouse to fly. Idle power also brakes on the ground.

      Helicopter on Medium or Hard: the joystick is the cyclic (tilt), and the second stick is the lever (up and down, it stays where you leave it) and the pedals (left and right).

      Push the joystick to fly. With Stick on Pilot, pull it down to climb; with Game, push it up. You can also drag on the screen. ▲ and ▼ change power, or go up and down in a hover. Hold FIRE to shoot; inside the barn it drops the supplies. Next ammo and Transform are above FIRE. To move the joystick to the other side, pick Left or Right under Joystick.

      The farmer is snowed in and low on food and supplies. We need to fly supplies to him as soon as possible. He can't hold out much longer.

      The farmer doesn't have anywhere to land, and wild animals will eat any food dropped outside the barn, so you have to fly through the barn and drop the boxes with your space bar. The farmer opened the barn doors for you.

      After you deliver the supplies, land back here at home base before the storm!
